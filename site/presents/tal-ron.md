# Tal Ron — The Gaming Lawyer from Tel Aviv

**The gaming lawyer who lives in brand paradise.**

Tal Itzhak Ron is a disruptive force in the iGaming industry, known for his exceptional energy and larger-than-life presence. An East European financial powerbroker once described him as someone who "could light the whole city with his energy."

> "You cannot stay indifferent to Tal Ron. You either love him from first sight or hate him until you learn to love him later."

## Professional Background

With 23 years of experience, Tal Ron has established himself as "the gaming lawyer" from Tel Aviv. The iGaming community affectionately refers to him as the "crazy genius gaming lawyer."

He holds five degrees and diplomas spanning computer science, mathematics, law, and music.

> "My strength is probably only 50% being the lawyer. The other 50% is like being a problem solver."

He has worked with major gaming companies since their inception, including Playtech (2003), MoonActive, SimilarWeb, and IronSource.

## Vision & Philosophy

Tal Ron describes himself as a "reality bender" — someone capable of making things happen uniquely. Rather than pursuing transactional relationships, he prioritizes community building.

> "I enjoy that the clients feel at home… and I really enjoy that our clients and our partners see this place as home."

He founded AI Hub, an Israeli incubator supporting industry companies.
