It’s hard enough to get noticed in iGaming. So if you’ve got a strong differentiator, flaunt it.

It’s easy to overlook Yaspa, an open banking provider that specializes in iGaming and high-risk industries, because it sounds just like every other one.

Fast. Secure. Seamless.

Pay-ins, payouts, account verification, virtual accounts.

All true, but also true for everyone else.

Yaspa isn’t really competing on speed or coverage.

Its real edge lies in something deeper. Intelligence capabilities.

Most open banking providers move money. Yaspa moves money and instantly reads what that money says about the player.

They are a money-mover, an AI security guard, and a business analyst all bundled together.

They move the money and give you a sneak peek into players’ financial status. Are they financially safe and not a compliance risk? A potential fraudster you should avoid? A valuable long-term player to nurture?

In high-risk, high-volume businesses like iGaming, where compliance, fraud, and customer value are critically linked to payments, that intelligence changes the game. Spot fraud before it costs you. Identify high-value players on their first deposit. Prove compliance in real-time.

But until Yaspa’s messaging reflects what they actually do differently, most operators will keep scrolling past the very solution they’ve been looking for.

When your product is smarter than your story, you leave money on the floor.
