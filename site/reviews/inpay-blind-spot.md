@image[/media/braintail/inpay-blind-spot-hero.webp | Inpay’s ironic blind spot is crippling its growth]

iGaming Payouts can send your players’ withdrawals to almost any bank account, in almost any country, in less than 5 minutes. That’s exceptional. No one else comes even close.

So why isn’t it growing at the pace its edge deserves?

Because the market doesn’t know what iGaming Payouts actually is.

If pay-ins are payments flowing from individuals (players, bettors, investors, buyers) to the business, then payouts flow in the opposite direction. Winnings. Withdrawals. Money back to the player. Payouts are different from pay-ins and require a different set of tools, expertise, and capabilities.

Over 17 years, iGaming Payouts built the vastest, fastest, and most reliable bank-to-bank payout network:

The vastest – offering payouts in 200+ countries.

The fastest – average payout time under 5 minutes.

The most reliable – 99.6% transaction success rate.

The most specialized – with deep iGaming expertise and compliance measures built for high-risk.

And, to make their offer even sweeter, iGaming Payouts also offers pay-ins for interested customers, although it doesn’t have much of an edge as it does in payouts.

Yet, the market doesn’t see how exceptional iGaming Payouts is.

iGaming Payouts’ sales team isn’t as extensive as its network. They do phenomenally well when they get in the room with clients, pitching their edge and showing their strength. But not enough people get in the room in the first place. Not enough people know of iGaming Payouts.

The company attends all the major industry events, and then some. But its constrained sales team competes for attention in a crowded payments market.

High-risk operators are already chased by providers in fancy booths and with big smiles. They all look generic, and iGaming Payouts gets lost in the sameness.

If only iGaming Payouts had a story that helped it stand out. If only its reputation and unfair advantage could travel farther than events, and reach clients faster than its salespeople can pitch.

Perhaps iGaming Payouts would have a better chance at standing out in the market if they didn’t call themselves the **payments people of Inpay.**

@image[/media/braintail/inpay-blind-spot-1.png | iGaming Payouts’ website]

iGaming Payouts is a fictitious name, but Inpay’s exceptional edge is real. If, like Inpay, your true worth is invisible in the market, it means your story doesn’t do you justice.

With a differentiated story, one that highlights your true edge, that’s relatable and easy to remember, you can reach farther and faster than your sales team.
